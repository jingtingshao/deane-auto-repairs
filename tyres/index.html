FAQ

Tyre questions

What’s included in the fitted tyre price?

Every price on this page is per tyre and includes the tyre, fitting, wheel balancing, GST and the Tyrewise fee.

How long does it take to fit new tyres?

A set of four usually takes about an hour once the tyres are here. If your tyres need to be ordered in, we’ll confirm when they arrive before you come in.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking is best so we can have your tyres ready before you arrive. Book online or call 0800 625 9827.

How do I find my tyre size?

Look on the side of your tyre for a code like 205/55R16 91V. 205 is the width in millimetres, 55 is the profile (sidewall height as a percentage of the width) and R16 is the rim size in inches. 91V is the load and speed rating — your new tyres should match or exceed it. The recommended size is usually also on the sticker inside the driver’s door.

Do you repair punctures?

Yes. Bring the car in and we’ll check whether the puncture can be repaired safely. If it can’t, we’ll show you why and give you a fitted price for a replacement before doing any work.
