Service guide

How often should you service?

  • Under 5 years: full service every 15,000 km or 12 months
  • 5 years+ / longer trips: full service every 10,000 km or 12 months
  • Older cars / short town trips: lube service every 5,000 km or 6 months

Regular servicing helps reduce long-term costs, improve performance, and lower the chance of an inconvenient breakdown.

No courtesy cars at this stage. You’re welcome to drop your vehicle off and arrange your own transport, or wait nearby if the job is short.
