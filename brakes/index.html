Signs your brakes need a check

  • Squealing or grinding when you brake
  • The car pulls to one side under braking
  • A soft or spongy brake pedal
  • Vibration through the pedal or steering wheel when braking
  • The brake warning light is on

Steering & suspension

Clunks over bumps, a car that wanders on the road or uneven tyre wear can point to worn steering or suspension parts. We’ll inspect it and quote before any work. If uneven wear has finished off your tyres, see fitted tyre prices online.

FAQ

Brake questions

How much do brake pads cost?

Brake pads start from $99 per axle (one pair, front or rear), including fitting. The final price depends on your car, so we’ll check it and give you a clear quote before any work starts.

How do I know if my brakes need checking?

Book a brake inspection if you hear squealing or grinding when you brake, the car pulls to one side, the pedal feels soft or spongy, you feel vibration when braking, or the brake warning light comes on.

Do you repair suspension?

Yes. We inspect steering and suspension, and suspension work is quoted after inspection — so you know the price before we start.

Will you do any work without asking me?

No. We explain what needs attention and what can wait, and additional work is only carried out with your approval.

Are brakes checked during a service?

Yes. A Standard service includes a wheels-on visual check of pads and discs. A Premium / Euro service includes a wheels-off brake inspection covering pads, discs, suspension and wheel bearings.
