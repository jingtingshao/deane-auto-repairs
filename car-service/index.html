FAQ

Servicing questions

How much is a car service?

Basic is $99, Standard is $199 and Premium / Euro is $299. Each includes an engine oil and standard oil filter change with up to 4L of standard-spec engine oil. Additional or specialised oil is quoted before the service.

What’s the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard adds safety checks to the oil change — fluids, battery, lights, tyres, steering, a wheels-on brake check, air filter, belts, wipers and exhaust — plus a digital service report. Premium / Euro adds a wheels-off brake inspection including suspension and wheel bearings, a brake fluid condition and cooling system check, a transmission and differential fluid level check where applicable, and a road test.

Do you service diesel and 4WD vehicles?

Yes. Diesel and 4WD servicing is from $299. Choose Diesel / 4WD service when you book, and we’ll confirm what’s included before work starts.

How long does a service take?

Standard and Premium services take about 1–2 hours, and you’re welcome to wait in the shop.

What is the digital service report?

Standard and Premium services include a detailed digital report you can open on your phone: a colour-coded checklist of what we checked, photos of anything worth watching, and clear recommendations. Keep it as your service history.

Can I get a WOF at the same time?

Yes. Choose Service + WOF when you book, and we’ll do both in the same visit. WOF inspections start from $79.

Where are you and when are you open?

63 Hayr Road, Three Kings, Auckland — next to the BP petrol station in Mt Roskill. Open Monday to Saturday, 9:30am – 5:00pm. Closed Sunday. Open in Google Maps
