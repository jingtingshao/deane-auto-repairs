Not sure which battery you need?

Put your car’s make, model and year (or your registration) in the booking form, or call 0800 625 9827. We’ll confirm the right battery and the price before fitting.

  • Most petrol cars — a Premium battery
  • Idle start-stop — usually an EFB or AGM battery

Signs your battery is going flat

  • A slow or laboured start
  • A clicking sound when you turn the key
  • Dim headlights or interior lights
  • The battery warning light is on
  • You’ve needed a jump start

Batteries often start to weaken after three or four years. A Standard service includes a battery test, so you’ll know before it lets you down.

FAQ

Battery questions

Is the battery test free?

Yes. We test your battery and your car’s charging system for free. If the battery is fine, there’s nothing to pay.

What’s included in the battery price?

Every price on this page is for one AA battery, fitted, including GST. Until 31 December 2026 every battery is 20% off the normal price.

Which battery does my car need?

Send us your car’s make, model and year (or the registration) through the booking form, or call 0800 625 9827. We’ll confirm the right battery and the price before fitting. Cars with idle start-stop usually need an EFB or AGM battery, and hybrids have their own 12V auxiliary battery.

How long does it take to fit a new battery?

Most batteries are fitted while you wait. If your battery needs to be ordered in, we’ll confirm when it arrives before you come in. Some European cars need the new battery registered to the car’s computer — we’ll tell you before we start if there’s any extra charge.

What warranty do the batteries have?

AA Premium batteries have a 3 year warranty. Extra Heavy Duty, EFB, AGM and Hybrid Auxiliary batteries have 2 years, and Commercial batteries have 1 year.

How do I know my battery is going flat?

Common signs are a slow or laboured start, a clicking sound when you turn the key, dim headlights, the battery warning light coming on, or a car that’s needed a jump start. Batteries often start to weaken after three or four years.
