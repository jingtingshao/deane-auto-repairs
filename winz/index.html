How a WINZ
quote works.

We inspect the vehicle, write a clear quote, and hand it back in a format that Work and Income can review.

  1. 01

    Book an inspection

    Tell us you need a WINZ quote when you book — by phone or the form on our website.

  2. 02

    We check the car

    We diagnose what’s needed for safety and roadworthiness — honest advice on what’s essential first.

  3. 03

    You get a written quote

    An itemised quote listing parts and labour, ready to give your case manager or submit with your application.

  4. 04

    Approve, then we repair

    Once Work and Income approves, bring the car back and we’ll complete the agreed work.

What you
receive.

No vague totals. A quote that shows the work, so you and Work and Income can see exactly what’s covered.

  • Itemised parts and labour
  • Clear description of each repair
  • Workshop name, address and GST details
  • Written format suitable for WINZ
  • Straight talk on what can wait

A note on
eligibility.

Work and Income decides who qualifies and how much help is available. That depends on your situation — we can’t approve or guarantee funding.

Start with Work and Income on 0800 559 009, then book with us for the quote they ask for. We’ll make that part simple.
