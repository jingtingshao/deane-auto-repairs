Conditions of inspection

This report describes the results of external examination of the vehicle, limited road testing, and visual inspection on the day shown above. It is supplied for the benefit of the report owner named on this document.

This inspection will not tell you about:

  • hidden defects
  • intermittent problems
  • problems which become apparent after the inspection
  • problems which cannot be identified on a visual inspection or without parts of the vehicle being dismantled

Component assemblies are not dismantled to measure internal parts; these are assessed by noise, operation and road performance where a road test is carried out. Vehicle condition can change after inspection due to wear, use, neglect, collision damage, or deterioration of internal components.

The 12V battery / charging assessment is limited to the 12V system and does not cover high-voltage systems on hybrid or electric vehicles. Road testing is limited (typically up to about 50 km/h unless stated) and only undertaken if the vehicle appears safe to test.

This report does not confirm vehicle ownership, finance, liens, or write-off history. It is not a Warrant of Fitness. Provided the inspection was carried out with reasonable care and skill, Deane Auto Repairs is not responsible for defects that were not identifiable in this limited inspection.
