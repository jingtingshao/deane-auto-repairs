What a WOF checks

A Warrant of Fitness is New Zealand’s regular safety check for your vehicle, carried out to NZ Transport Agency (NZTA) standards. It covers things like:

  • Tyre tread depth and condition
  • Brakes
  • Lights and indicators
  • Steering and suspension
  • Seatbelts and airbags
  • Windscreen, wipers and washers
  • Rust in structural areas, doors and body
  • Exhaust and fuel leaks

FAQ

WOF questions

How much is a WOF?

A WOF is $79 for cars and $89 for light commercial vehicles (vans and utes). If your vehicle needs any repairs to pass, we’ll give you a clear quote before any work starts.

What happens if my car fails its WOF?

We’ll show you what didn’t pass and why, in plain language, and quote the repairs before doing anything. Nothing is fixed without your approval. If your car doesn’t pass, we’ll re-inspect it free within 28 days.

Can I get a WOF and a service in the same visit?

Yes. Choose Service + WOF when you book online, or call 0800 625 9827, and we’ll do both in the same visit.

How do I know when my WOF is due?

The expiry date is on the WOF label on the inside of your windscreen. How often you need a WOF depends on how old your vehicle is and when it was first registered.

Do I need to book a WOF?

Yes. WOFs are by booking only, so please book before you come in. Book online on this page or call 0800 625 9827. Tell us your registration and a preferred time, and we’ll get back to you to confirm.
